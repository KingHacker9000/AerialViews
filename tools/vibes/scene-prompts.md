# Starter Vibe Pack Prompts

These are deliberately written for a **locked projector window** look: no people, no text, no camera movement, and only slow motion that can survive an all-night loop.

## Shared SDXL negative prompt

Use this negative prompt for every seed image:

`people, person, human, text, letters, logo, watermark, signature, UI, frame, border, fisheye, dutch angle, tilted horizon, blurry, low detail, oversaturated, deformed architecture, duplicated windows, impossible geometry, daylight, camera motion`

For FramePack, keep motion prompts short and motion-focused. If it starts moving the camera, explicitly add `The camera remains completely locked in place.`

## 01 — Snowy Gothic Window

**Seed image**

`cinematic night view from inside a dark gothic stone arcade, three tall pointed cathedral arches opening toward a vast distant city covered in warm lights, snow resting on the stone sill, pine branches at the far left, bright moon in a deep blue winter sky, atmospheric haze, realistic stone, subtle volumetric moonlight, dark foreground, cozy distant lights, fantasy realism, extremely detailed, symmetrical locked-off composition, 16:9`

**Motion**

`Gentle snow falls continuously outside the arches, distant city lights twinkle softly, thin clouds drift very slowly past the moon, pine branches sway slightly. The stone architecture stays perfectly still and the camera remains completely locked in place.`

## 02 — Rainy Tokyo Apartment

**Seed image**

`dark cozy modern apartment at night seen from several meters back, huge floor-to-ceiling window covered with rain droplets, dense Tokyo skyline outside with soft neon signs and distant traffic lights, warm dim lamp reflection inside, blue and amber palette, photorealistic cinematic lighting, uncluttered room, no people, locked-off wide composition, 16:9`
